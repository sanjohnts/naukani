About the Project

Nadukani Zoo & Safari Park is a proposed Kerala Government wildlife tourism project, planned to become a major wildlife destination in North Kerala.

Project background

An ambitious project for North Kerala

Nadukani Zoo & Safari Park is an ambitious proposed tourism project planned in the beautiful surroundings of Nadukani, Taliparamba. The project aims to create an integrated wildlife experience with a zoo, safari, botanical garden, natural history museum and biodiversity park.

The park was announced as a major tourism development project in the 2024 Kerala Budget, which proposed a full-fledged safari park at Nadukani with a museum, zoo and biodiversity park. The project site is associated with Plantation Corporation land in the Nadukani division, along the Taliparamba–Alakode State Highway corridor.

The vision is to promote wildlife awareness, nature education and responsible tourism while developing the region as a major tourism destination.

History

Project milestones

Key steps reported between 2024 and 2026.

All news & updates

  1. February 2024

    Announced in the Kerala Budget

    The Kerala Budget proposed a full-fledged safari park at Nadukani, with a museum, zoo and biodiversity park.

  2. July 2024

    Approval and land transfer

    Government-level approval and land-transfer procedures were reported. Approximately 250–257 acres were identified or transferred from Plantation Corporation land.

  3. October 2025

    Expert committee for the DPR

    A five-member expert committee was formed to prepare the Detailed Project Report (DPR). The reported project area was 252.8 acres.

  4. February – March 2026

    Technical survey tender

    Tender activity related to a technical survey for the proposed park was reported.

  5. 8 March 2026 Latest

    Camp office inaugurated

    A camp office for the proposed zoo-safari project was inaugurated.
