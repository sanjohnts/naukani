Native Western Ghats biodiversity

The plants, insects, birds and animals native to the Western Ghats, one of the richest biodiversity regions on Earth.

Native focus
Representative image
Western Ghats

A biodiversity hotspot on the doorstep

Kannur district stretches from the Arabian Sea coast to the slopes of the Western Ghats, a mountain range recognised as one of the world's biodiversity hotspots and inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012.

The Ghats are home to a remarkable number of plants and animals found nowhere else on Earth. Showcasing and explaining this native biodiversity is a natural focus for a wildlife destination in North Kerala.

  • Evergreen and moist deciduous forests
  • Endemic plants, amphibians, birds and insects
  • Rivers and streams that begin in the hills
Good practice

Responsible wildlife viewing

General guidance followed at zoos and safari parks everywhere. Official park rules will be published by the park authority.

Watch quietlyLow voices and slow movements help animals stay relaxed and visible.
Never feed animalsOutside food can make animals ill and change their natural behaviour.
Respect barriersBarriers and distances exist to protect both animals and visitors.
Leave no traceTake your litter with you and keep plastic out of natural habitats.
