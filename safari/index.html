Proposed safari concept

The Safari Experience

A safari experience in which visitors would travel through naturalistic animal habitats in specially protected vehicles.

Representative image
The concept

Travel through the wild

In a safari park, animals live in large, open, naturalistic enclosures, and visitors move through those habitats in vehicles built for safety. It turns the conventional zoo around: instead of walking past enclosures, visitors enter the animals' landscape.

Nadukani's safari is planned around this idea. Details of the vehicles, route, duration and charges will be shared once they are officially announced.

Safety on safari

  • Stay seated inside the vehicle at all times
  • Keep windows and doors as instructed by staff
  • Never feed, call out to or tease animals
  • Keep noise low and follow your guide
  • Follow all official park rules once they are published

General safari-park practice. These are not official Nadukani rules.

How it works

A typical safari park visit

An illustration of how safari parks generally work. Nadukani's visitor flow will be confirmed in official plans.

01

Arrive & orient

Visitors usually begin at a reception area with information on habitats and safety.

02

Board a protected vehicle

Guided vehicles carry visitors safely into the habitat zones.

03

Travel through habitats

Animals are observed in large, naturalistic spaces rather than small enclosures.

04

Explore on foot

Many parks pair the safari with walk-through areas such as museums and gardens.
