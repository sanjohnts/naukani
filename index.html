About the project

A wildlife destination rooted in nature

Nadukani Zoo & Safari Park is an ambitious proposed tourism project planned in the beautiful surroundings of Nadukani, Taliparamba. The project aims to create an integrated wildlife experience with a zoo, safari, botanical garden, natural history museum and biodiversity park.

The vision is to promote wildlife awareness, nature education and responsible tourism while developing the region as a major tourism destination in North Kerala.
