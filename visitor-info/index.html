Getting here

Reaching Taliparamba

General travel information. Distances and directions will be added once the official entrance is confirmed.

By airKannur International Airport at Mattannur is the nearest airport.
By railKannur and Payyanur are the nearest major railway stations to Taliparamba.
By roadTaliparamba lies on National Highway 66. Nadukani is along the Taliparamba–Alakode State Highway.
